## Brand & Style

This design system translates the legendary taste profile of layered chocolate, roasted nuts, gooey caramel, and nougat into a decadent, high-conversion direct-to-consumer e-commerce experience. The aesthetic balances heritage indulgence with contemporary artisanal luxury.

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
- **Appetizing & Tactile:** Surfaces evoke natural confectionery ingredients—deep roasted cocoa, whipped nougat, and glossy molten caramel.
- **Punchy & Confident:** High visual contrast pairs athletic blue and golden caramel against rich roasted espresso browns, projecting timeless authority rather than delicate fragility.
- **Refined Indulgence:** Every element feels substantial, premium, and crafted. The design avoids sterile clinical minimalism, favoring rich organic warmth and tactile responsiveness.

### Target Audience
Confectionery connoisseurs, gift buyers, and nostalgic indulgence seekers looking for elevated, small-batch, or iconic confectionery experiences on mobile and desktop.

### Design Style
A synthesis of **Tactile Warmth** and **Bold Modern Editorial**:
- Generous micro-spacing and chunky pill-shaped affordances.
- Deep cocoa contrast framing luscious food photography.
- Diffused, warm-tinted shadows that simulate physical packaging on warm marble counters.

## Colors

The palette directly references the anatomical layers of the iconic bar: roasted chocolate shell, flowing golden caramel, whipped nougat core, and high-impact athletic blue branding.

### Functional Palette Mapping
- **Primary (`#2D1606` Cocoa Espresso):** Primary CTA fills, display headlines, structural borders, and dark hero banners. Conveys richness and solid depth.
- **Deep Obsidian Cocoa (`#1A0C03`):** High-depth contrast for footer environments, modal backdrops, and active-state button fills.
- **Secondary (`#E59B2C` Salted Caramel):** Attention accents, secondary CTA highlights, discount banners, rating stars, and interactive focus states.
- **Secondary Light (`#F5A623` Golden Drizzle):** Micro-interaction highlights, hover states, and promotional banner accents.
- **Tertiary (`#004B87` Heritage Royal Blue):** Expressive brand accent reserved for "Limited Edition" callouts, trust badges, shipping ribbons, and active filters.
- **Tertiary Vibrant (`#0056B3` Athletic Blue):** Dynamic link hovers, cart notification dots, and vibrant interactive badges.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FDFBF7` Whipped Nougat):** Base background tone for maximum readability and appetizing warmth.
- **Surface Container (`#F7F2EB` Roasted Cream):** Secondary background cards, input field fills, item shelves, and modular content separators.
- **Crisp Neutral (`#FFFFFF` Pure White):** Product card backgrounds, active dialogs, and pill toggle surfaces to lift key items off the nougat canvas.

## Typography

The typographic pairing unites the sturdy, mechanical confidence of geometric grotesque display headers with friendly, hyper-legible humanist body text.

### Pairing Strategy
- **Space Grotesk (Display & Headlines):** Chunky, architectural, and robust. Used for section titles, product names, price displays, and key marketing headlines. Its distinct geometric cuts provide energy and boldness without feeling retro.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body, Utility, & Labels):** Softened neo-grotesque curves offering warm readability. Perfect for ingredient profiles, tasting notes, checkout forms, and pill label metadata.

### Typographic Rules
- Set primary headlines with tight line heights and negative letter-spacing for a compact, poster-like impact.
- Badges and micro-labels (`label-sm`) default to uppercase with `0.05em` tracking for crisp legibility on colored fills.
- High-contrast numbers: Space Grotesk is leveraged for currency amounts and weights to impart an industrial confectionery feel.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt base grid with a fluid responsive container architecture, prioritizing large-format product visuals, appetizing negative space, and unhindered thumb navigation on touch devices.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout, `3rem` (48px) margins, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Maximum content width: 1360px.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` (32px) margins, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout, `1.25rem` (20px) margins, `1rem` (16px) gutters. Sticky bottom CTA bar for mobile checkout actions.

### Spacing Density Principles
- **Macro Spacing:** Use `space-xl` and double increments for editorial sections to keep the visual tone airy and luxurious.
- **Micro Spacing:** Keep internal component padding snug (`space-sm` to `space-md`) within cards to emphasize compact packaging aesthetics.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through buttery, warm ambient lighting rather than generic cool grays. Surfaces feel physical, casting soft shadows as if resting on a kitchen table.

### Ambient Shadow Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat):** Neutral background panels (`#F7F2EB`) and subtle form inputs with crisp border containment.
- **Level 1 (Card Rest):** `0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(45, 22, 6, 0.06), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(45, 22, 6, 0.04)`. Gives pure white cards a gentle separation from the creamy nougat backdrop.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Floating Elements):** `0px 12px 28px -4px rgba(45, 22, 6, 0.12), 0px 4px 10px -2px rgba(45, 22, 6, 0.06)`. Used for hovered product cards, dropdown flyouts, and sticky navigation headers.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Quick-Cart Slideouts):** `0px 24px 48px -8px rgba(26, 12, 3, 0.22), 0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(45, 22, 6, 0.08)`. Ensures immediate focus on shopping carts and transaction dialogues.

### Lighting & Tinting
All shadows use primary cocoa (`#2D1606`) or deep obsidian (`#1A0C03`) as their alpha base rather than pure black, maintaining visual warmth across all lighting contexts.

## Shapes

The shape system pairs generous, friendly curves with structured silhouettes, echoing smooth chocolate shells and soft candy centers.

### Curvature Hierarchy
- **Standard Cards & Modules (`rounded-2xl` / 1.5rem):** Applied to product cards, reviews containers, and checkout summaries to soften structural layouts.
- **Containers & Drawers (`rounded-xl` / 1rem):** Used for filter panels, form blocks, and banner promotions.
- **Pill Geometry (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Applied strictly to interactive controls: buttons, badges, category filters, nutritional chips, and counter steppers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), deep cocoa fill (`#2D1606`), crisp white text, bold label styling. On hover: shifts to Obsidian Cocoa (`#1A0C03`) with a slight upward translation (`-1px`).
- **Accent Button (Add to Cart / Buy Now):** Rich Caramel fill (`#E59B2C`), deep cocoa text (`#2D1606`), tactile active press state.
- **Ghost / Outline Button:** 1.5px solid border in `#2D1606`, transparent background, dark cocoa text. Hover activates soft caramel fill with 10% opacity.

### Badges & Chips
- **Limited Edition / Special Feature Badges:** Pill-shaped, classic royal athletic blue (`#004B87`) fill with bold white typography.
- **Flavor & Dietary Chips:** Soft cream fill (`#F7F2EB`), subtle cocoa border, cocoa text. In active state: caramel fill (`#E59B2C`) with dark cocoa text.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Solid `#FFFFFF` container with `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem) corners and Level 1 elevation.
- **Image Frame:** Top 65% area featuring a soft, warm nougat inset (`#F7F2EB`) with a subtle `rounded-xl` internal radius.
- **Card Content:** Category chip, bold Space Grotesk product title, weight/pack count in subdued cocoa, price highlighted with `headline-sm`, and an inline circular or pill "Add" trigger.

### Form Inputs
- Background in white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1.5px border in `#E8DFD3`.
- Focus state transition: border color shifts cleanly to royal blue (`#004B87`) with a 3px ring in `rgba(0, 75, 135, 0.15)`. Pill or `rounded-xl` radius.

### Steppers & Quantity Selectors
- Compact pill container with `#F7F2EB` fill. Cocoa minus/plus icon buttons with bold counter typography centered between them.
